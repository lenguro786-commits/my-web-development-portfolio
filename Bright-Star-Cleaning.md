# Bright Star Cleaning — Business Website

**Live website:** https://musical-bombolone-80f983.netlify.app/

## Overview
Bright Star Cleaning is a business website concept designed to present cleaning services clearly and professionally to potential customers.

## Project Goals
- Create a professional online presence for a cleaning business
- Present services in a clear, accessible layout
- Make the website easy to navigate on desktop and mobile
- Help prospective customers find information about the business

## Technologies
- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript

## Purpose
This project demonstrates my front-end web development practice in building responsive business websites with clear layouts, user-friendly navigation, and a professional presentation.
